Constraints

Display

  • Waveshare 4.2 V2 only (V2 sticker, SSD1683 controller): V1 (UC8176) is not compatible with the firmware.
  • Board 103.5 × 78.5, holes 97 × 72.5 center to center. Remove the brass standoffs from the back side - the display is screwed directly into the frame.
  • Insert the JST PH 8 plug before mounting into the frame: it is a side connector, unreachable after mounting. The plug is no higher than 7.5 above the board and no further than 2 beyond the right edge.
  • The display goes into the frame from below. The wires run to the left above the corner bracket into the back panel window.
  • Do not bend the glass flex cable toward the front or at a right angle.

XIAO ESP32-C6

  • USB-C in the right wall: opening 10 × 4.5, center 33.5 from the front edge of the bottom and 7 from the underside of the enclosure.
  • The board rests on two supports of the bottom under its middle, held in place by the connector in the wall and by stops on the antenna side. Wires are soldered to the edge pads on both sides.
  • BAT+/BAT- pads are on the underside: a 0.5 recess in the bottom beneath them.

Battery

  • 1S Li-Ion/LiPo only with a protection board (PCM): the XIAO does not cut off deep discharge.
  • Compartment 70 × 54, height to the ceiling 12.5. Capacity 2000-4000 mAh, length including PCM no more than 70.
  • PCM - toward the right rib, facing the XIAO; wires 35.

Button module

  • Snap off the break-off rail 3 with the fiducial mark: with it the board is 18.5 and does not fit.
  • The Ø3 holes, 49 × 9.5 center to center, sit on Ø2 pins in the bottom. Pads V G 1 2 3 4 face forward, toward the XIAO.
  • Buttons 6 × 6 with a Ø3.5 plunger, pitch 12.5. The cap rests on the plunger: Ø4 socket 1.5 deep. With a different button height the cap will sit higher or lower by the same difference.
  • Order front to back: 4, 3, 2, 1. Wires 1-4 to D0-D3 no longer than 35.

Antenna

  • Glued to the cover ceiling in a 40 × 20 zone (40-80 from the left edge, 25-45 from the front edge of the bottom), not above the battery.
  • Cable 80, ~15 needed. Select the external antenna in the firmware (GPIO14 HIGH).

Fasteners and printing

  • Screws are countersunk M2.5 only (head 4). Length limits are in the fasteners table.
  • Printing without supports, walls from 1.5, shelf at countersinks from 1.5, 0.4 chamfer on edges at the bed.
  • Export the frame and the lettering as a single 3MF and in Bambu Studio choose one object made of several parts.
