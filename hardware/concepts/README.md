# Mechanical concepts

These are design studies, not parts of the released enclosure. Build and print the
device from [Rev 1](../step/rev1/) and the [assembly guide](https://perinkpheral.dev/hardware).

- [Adjustable battery bridge](adjustable-battery-bridge.html): a standalone interactive
  drawing of a slotted bridge on two fixed screws. Open it in a browser. Its positions
  are illustrative; dimensions, clearances and printability have not been qualified.

The production STEP and 3MF files stay in `hardware/step/rev1/`. A concept becomes a
production part only after the model, exports and assembly instructions agree.
