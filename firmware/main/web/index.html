Restart and reset

Erasing forgets the Wi-Fi network, every setting and everything the applications stored. The device comes back with its own access point, waiting to be set up.

Settings

What the device calls itself, on its setup network too. On the local network it answers to -.

A POSIX time zone, the way the device keeps it. The button fills in the zone of the computer or phone this page is open on.

A quick refresh leaves a faint trace of the picture before; a full one blinks and wipes it. The screen is always wiped when another application takes it.

How often a clock or a forecast may change the picture by itself. A press of a button is always answered at once. Fewer refreshes, longer life of the panel.

The panel is black and white. With this on, an application's grey is a fine pattern of both; off, it is whichever of the two is nearer.

The device restarts and joins this network. If it cannot, its own access point comes back after two minutes.

About

perINKpheral is a desk e-paper companion: a screen and four buttons that live on a shelf and show what you glance at. The device does the work itself - it fetches the data, runs the application and draws the frame - with no server of anybody's in between.
